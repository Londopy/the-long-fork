THE LONG FORK - RULES

1. Fork the current tip, not the root. The tip is listed in the root
   repo's STATUS.txt.
2. Do not rename the repo. (Keeps the chain easy to follow.)
3. Make exactly one commit: append one line to CHAIN.txt.
4. Line format:
     <depth> | <github-username> | <YYYY-MM-DD> | <prev-hash> | <cell> | <note>
   Depth = previous line's depth + 1.
   prev-hash = first 12 hex characters of the SHA-256 of the previous
   line's full text (no trailing newline).
   cell = x,y=c sets one cell of the shared 64 x 32 canvas (x is 0-63,
   y is 0-31, c is one printable ASCII character other than a space
   or |), or - to skip.
   Note is optional, max 80 characters, plain ASCII only.
5. Commit message format:
     link <depth>: <github-username>
6. Do not change any other file.
7. Do not delete your fork. Deleting a link breaks the chain for
   everyone after you.
8. One link per human. No alt accounts, no throwaway orgs.
9. Keep notes clean. No links, no ads, no slurs. Offending links
   get excluded from the official count.

Example CHAIN.txt:
  0 | Londopy | 2026-09-24 | 000000000000 | - | root
  1 | someuser | 2026-09-25 | 766aac5a6391 | 31,15=@ | hi
  2 | anotheruser | 2026-09-25 | dfd786cf1f66 | 32,15=# |
